# SuppClarity interface direction

Updated 21 November 2026 after the first visual direction was rejected as generic.

## References and practical lessons

These are established subscription products, not a verified ranking of the highest-revenue apps. Revenue does not establish that a particular screen causes conversion.

- MyFitnessPal Today: prioritize useful actions and the person's recorded information. https://support.myfitnesspal.com/hc/en-us/articles/39985611667341-Your-Today-tab
- MyFitnessPal plan comparison: describe paid value concretely rather than filling the interface with generic AI promises. https://support.myfitnesspal.com/hc/en-us/articles/34889191368077-The-difference-between-Free-Premium-and-Premium
- Flo: a short goal-based entry and optional premium content, while retaining the essentials. https://help.flo.health/hc/en-us/articles/4406826484500-Setting-up-your-Flo-account ; https://help.flo.health/hc/en-us/articles/360042141812-What-is-included-in-Flo-Premium
- Calm: a clear recurring content proposition. A supplement app must demonstrate its own repeat-use value rather than copy a meditation streak. https://support.calm.com/hc/en-us/articles/360008536834-Calm-Premium-vs-Free-Features-Content-List-Benefits
- RevenueCat's 2026 subscription report: use category benchmarks to frame experiments, not as a revenue forecast. https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps

## Design decisions

Use a compact distinctive logo, Manrope typography, neutral surfaces, iris and coral accents and a strong primary task. Remove the decorative pill hero. The initial screen offers a personal starting check, direct label entry and the current routine. Returned users see their actual entry count and actual overlapping ingredient count, without invented health scores. Keep evidence optional and material limitations visible.

One question per screen, a visible progress line and a pause-and-exit action support interrupted use. Reading sizes scale custom text overrides as well as body text. Labels and quantities are confirmed before arithmetic. Users can explore without a bottle, sign-in, payment card or wearable.

Do not introduce manipulative countdowns, paid safety warnings, fabricated social proof, prescription changes, an unsupported AI recommendation or a claimed personalized deficiency from a short questionnaire. The current preview is free, with planned membership clearly identified as future functionality.

## Logo

A compact intertwined C and S with one coral focal point, designed for a small app header and icon. The mark is a working brand concept and has not undergone trademark clearance. Created with the built-in image generation tool, then refined to remove glow and visual noise. The generation prompt requested a rounded geometric C/S monogram, solid iris #6955DF, one coral #F66B4E dot, true transparency, and no medical cross, leaf, shield, checkmark or text. Assets are saved in the app's assets folder.
