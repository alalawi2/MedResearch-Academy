Free and optional Plus

SuppClarity provides general supplement education for adults 18 and older. Free tools remain available without subscribing. Plus adds the features and request allowances displayed before purchase. It does not provide diagnosis, treatment, personalized prescribing or clinical validation.

Price and renewal

Your Apple App Store or Google Play checkout shows the applicable local price, billing period and taxes before you confirm. The monthly plan renews each month; the yearly plan renews each year until cancelled. Payment is charged to your store account. The current Plus plans do not include a free trial.

Manage, cancel and restore

Cancel through your App Store or Google Play subscription settings before the next renewal. Deleting the app or clearing entries does not cancel billing. Access normally continues to the end of the paid period unless the store revokes or refunds it. Use Restore purchases with the same store account after reinstalling. Cross-platform transfer is not included.

Online allowances

Plus includes 100 photo scans and 300 online AI answers per calendar month, counted in UTC. Daily limits are 20 photo scans and 50 online answers. Unused requests do not roll over. Requests accepted for provider processing count even if the provider cannot finish them. Name search, local prepared answers and reference guides have separate service availability protections. Internet access and functioning third-party services are needed for online features.

Your entries

Saved products and routine history remain accessible if Plus ends. Reminders pause and may need reopening the app to refresh. Alerts are best effort and are not a medical alarm. The free text export remains available. You control whether to generate and share a PDF.

Support and refunds

Contact info@medresearch-academy.om for app or subscription support. The relevant store handles billing and refund requests under its rules and applicable consumer rights. Nothing here removes mandatory consumer rights.
