A little help.

Find a bottle

Start with a clear front photo or search the brand and full product name. Add the printed strength when you know it. We check NIH labels and, if needed, online product sources. Confirm the country, formula and amounts against your bottle before saving. A second photo is only needed when the ingredients cannot be confirmed.

Camera access

If access is off, use the camera settings button on the photo screen, enable Camera for SuppClarity and return. You can also choose an existing photo, search by name or enter the label manually. In Expo Go, permissions belong to Expo Go.

What does the amount mean?

Tap Daily amount & more beside an ingredient for its function and, where available, general daily references. Those are food-and-supplement references, not a prescription to take the remaining amount. Caffeine has no daily requirement.

Your routine

The installed app saves confirmed entries on your device. Your space has export, reading-size and clear-data controls. The browser preview clears on reload. Considering a product keeps it separate from the amounts you currently take.
